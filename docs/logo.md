# School logo: Základní škola Maltézských rytířů

**Status: proposal, needs approval** by the school's management and by the founder (Suverénní řád maltézských rytířů – České velkopřevorství). The Grand Magistry should confirm the use of the shield (see "Approvals" below).

## Basis

The logo follows the lockup of the Order's school **Vyšší odborná škola zdravotnická** (vos.mojezdravka.cz, `logo-vos-male2.png`): the red shield of the Order's works on the left, and the school's name in two lines of a geometric sans-serif, in the Order's red, beside it and aligned to the shield's lower half.

Nothing of the emblem is redrawn. The shield (white ground, red outline, red field, white eight-pointed cross) is the **vector from the Grand Magistry master lockup** that maltezskyrad.cz already uses (`maltezskyrad/static/img/lockup/*.svg`, paths 0–7, red `#e31818`), copied unchanged into `tools/shield.svg`.

Type: **TeX Gyre Adventor Bold** (GUST Font Licence), the stand-in for Century Gothic that the Coat of Arms and Emblems Guidelines name for the Order's lockups. It is the same substitute used for the maltezskyrad.cz lockups. Text is converted to outlines, so the files need no font.

The files are generated by `tools/make_logo.py` (fontTools). Re-run it to change the wording; never edit the SVGs by hand.

All SVGs scale without loss. For print, export at the required size from the SVG (e.g. Inkscape, `rsvg-convert -w 2000`).

## Rules

- **Clear space:** keep free space around the logo at least the width of one arm of the cross (≈ 1/4 of the shield width) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** main logo 32 mm / 140 px wide; compact logo 18 mm / 80 px; shield alone 6 mm / 24 px tall.
- **Colours:** red `#e31818` (the Order's web red; take print values from the Coat of Arms and Emblems Guidelines) and white; or entirely black; or entirely white. No other colours, gradients, shadows, outlines or effects.
- **Backgrounds:** the colour logo goes on white or very light, plain backgrounds. On photographs or dark colours, use the negative version.
- **Do not** redraw, stretch, rotate or crop the shield; change the cross; set the name in another font; rearrange the shield and the text; or animate the logo.
- **Do not** combine the logo with the Order's armorial bearing (the institutional emblem with the princely crown). It belongs to the Order's institutions, not to its works.
- The name is always written "Základní škola Maltézských rytířů" (as in the school register). The short form "ZŠMR" appears only in the compact logo.

## Approvals and open points

1. **School and founder:** approval of the design and the wording ("Základní škola / Maltézských rytířů"; whether "Kladno" belongs in the main version).
2. **Grand Magistry (CoA guidelines):** the red shield with the white cross is the emblem of the Order's activities and works. Its use by a school founded by the Grand Priory follows the VOS precedent, but the Communications Office should confirm it, together with the font substitute (TeX Gyre Adventor for Century Gothic).
3. **Negative version:** whether the Grand Magistry allows a white shield with a dark cross, or requires the full-colour shield on a white field on dark backgrounds.
4. **Žerotínova school:** the brief names the Maltese school in Žerotínova (Prague) as the primary reference. Its repository (`skolaPraha`) was empty during this work, so the logo follows the VOS lockup that the client supplied. Align the two once the Prague logo exists.
