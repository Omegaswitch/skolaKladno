# School logo: Základní škola Maltézských rytířů

**Status: proposal, needs approval** by the school's management and by the founder (Suverénní řád maltézských rytířů – České velkopřevorství). The Grand Magistry should confirm the use of the shield (see "Approvals" below).

## Basis

The logo rebuilds the lockup of the Order's school **Vyšší odborná škola zdravotnická** (vos.mojezdravka.cz, `logo-vos-male2.png`), measured from that file: the Order's official Czech lockup (red shield + **MALTÉZSKÝ ŘÁD / ČESKÁ REPUBLIKA**), a hairline rule under it with a short tick down at its left end, and below the rule the school's name in two lines of the Order's red. Only the school's name is new.

Nothing of the emblem is redrawn. The shield (white ground, red outline, red field, white eight-pointed cross) is the **vector from the Grand Magistry master lockup** that maltezskyrad.cz already uses (`maltezskyrad/static/img/lockup/*.svg`, paths 0–7, red `#e31818`), copied unchanged into `tools/shield.svg`; the words "MALTÉZSKÝ ŘÁD / ČESKÁ REPUBLIKA" are path 8 of the same official file (`cs.svg`), copied unchanged into `tools/order-lockup-cs.svg`.

Type of the school's name: **TeX Gyre Adventor Bold** (GUST Font Licence), the stand-in for Century Gothic that the Coat of Arms and Emblems Guidelines name for the Order's lockups. It is the same substitute used for the maltezskyrad.cz lockups. Text is converted to outlines, so the files need no font.

The files are generated by `tools/make_logo.py` (fontTools). Re-run it to change the wording; never edit the SVGs by hand.

## Files (`static/img/logo/`)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `zsmr-logo.svg` | **Main logo, light grounds.** Letterheads, documents, signage. "MALTÉZSKÝ ŘÁD / ČESKÁ REPUBLIKA" and the rule in black (emblem text is black in the Order's guidelines), name in red. |
| `zsmr-logo-compact.svg` | **Secondary, compact:** shield + "ZŠMR". Narrow spaces, social profile banners, stamps of class projects. |
| `zsmr-mark.svg` | Shield alone: favicon (SVG), app icon, very small sizes. Use it only where the school's name is legible nearby. |
| `zsmr-logo-photo.svg` | **Photographs and dark grounds** (the website header), as the VOS original: full-colour shield with its white edge, "MALTÉZSKÝ ŘÁD / ČESKÁ REPUBLIKA" and the rule in white, name in red. Use it only on a ground dark enough for the white words. |
| `zsmr-logo-mono-black.svg` | **Monochrome:** one-colour black print (fax, stamps, engraving, black-and-white newsletters). |
| `zsmr-logo-mono-white.svg` | **Negative, one colour:** everything white, for one-colour white printing on dark material. The cross is drawn in black; ask the printer to knock it out. |
| `zsmr-og.png` | 1200×630 preview image for links shared on social networks. |

All SVGs scale without loss. For print, export at the required size from the SVG (e.g. Inkscape, `rsvg-convert -w 2000`).

## Rules

- **Clear space:** keep free space around the logo at least the width of one arm of the cross (≈ 1/4 of the shield width) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** main logo 40 mm / 180 px wide ("ČESKÁ REPUBLIKA" must stay legible); compact logo 18 mm / 80 px; shield alone 6 mm / 24 px tall.
- **Colours:** red `#e31818` (the Order's web red; take print values from the Coat of Arms and Emblems Guidelines) and white; or entirely black; or entirely white. No other colours, gradients, shadows, outlines or effects.
- **Backgrounds:** `zsmr-logo.svg` on white or very light, plain grounds; `zsmr-logo-photo.svg` on photographs and dark grounds. Never the white-text version on white (its words disappear), never the black-text version on a photograph.
- **Do not** redraw, stretch, rotate or crop the shield; change the cross; alter "MALTÉZSKÝ ŘÁD / ČESKÁ REPUBLIKA"; set the name in another font; rearrange the parts; or animate the logo.
- **Do not** combine the logo with the Order's armorial bearing (the institutional emblem with the princely crown). It belongs to the Order's institutions, not to its works.
- The name is always written "Základní škola Maltézských rytířů" (as in the school register). The short form "ZŠMR" appears only in the compact logo.

## Approvals and open points

1. **School and founder:** approval of the design and the wording ("Základní škola / Maltézských rytířů"; the place name is not in the logo, as on the VOS logo).
2. **Grand Magistry (CoA guidelines):** the red shield with the white cross is the emblem of the Order's activities and works. Its use by a school founded by the Grand Priory follows the VOS precedent, but the Communications Office should confirm it, together with the font substitute (TeX Gyre Adventor for Century Gothic).
3. **Negative version:** whether the Grand Magistry allows a white shield with a dark cross, or requires the full-colour shield on a white field on dark backgrounds.
4. **Žerotínova school:** the brief names the Maltese school in Žerotínova (Prague) as the primary reference. Its repository (`skolaPraha`) was empty during this work, so the logo follows the VOS lockup that the client supplied as the reference for the Order's schools. Align the two once the Prague logo exists.
