# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static site, same stack as maltezskyrad.cz: HTML/CSS generated by a Python 3 standard-library build script, content as files in this repository, two small Vercel Node functions (news publishing, trust box). No framework, no CMS, no client-side framework.

## Users

Primary: parents of current and prospective pupils (looking for documents, fees, timetables, contacts, news of their child's class), pupils, and teachers who publish news. Secondary: applicants for teaching posts, the founder (the Czech Grand Priory of the Order of Malta, presenting the site to the Grand Prior), the town of Kladno and funding bodies.

## Product Purpose

The website of Základní škola Maltézských rytířů in Kladno – Kročehlavy, a Christian primary school (all nine years) founded by the Sovereign Order of Malta – Czech Grand Priory. It informs families, keeps the school's documents and news in one place, and shows the school as a work of the Order. Success: a parent finds a form, a fee or a phone number in seconds; a teacher publishes news without help; the Grand Prior recognises the site as part of the Order's web family.

## Positioning

A small church school with small classes, open to children from believing and non-believing families, running since September 1991 (all facts from the school's own profile page).

## Operating Context

- Previous site: WordPress at zsmr.eu (21 pages, 436 posts from 2017 to 2026, about 3,100 media files), plain permalinks.
- Sister sites: maltezskyrad.cz (Grand Priory), velkoprevorskypalac.eu, lurdy.eu; the Order's other schools (VOŠZ, vos.mojezdravka.cz, whose logo pattern this site follows).
- Governing references: the Order's Communications Policy (Feb 2025) and Coat of Arms and Emblems Guidelines (v3.3).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Czech only.
- Every old URL keeps working through a permanent redirect.
- Shield of works only; no armorial bearing; no new emblems.
- No invented facts; imported texts unchanged and marked for review.
- Teachers publish news through `/pridat-aktualitu/` (the maltezskyrad.cz editor).

## Brand Commitments

Same as maltezskyrad.cz: #e31a18 / #f3f3f3 / #c7c7c7 / #454545, Asap, EB Garamond headings (pending confirmation), basic image 800×470.

## Product Principles

1. Families first: documents, fees, timetables and contacts never more than one click from the home page.
2. One Order: the founder and orderofmalta.int are visible on every page.
3. Truth over polish: preserve the school's own words and photographs; flag what is outdated.
4. Easy to keep alive: any teacher can publish an article in a minute.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Parents on phones, grandparents with large text, keyboard and screen-reader users; reduced motion respected.
